# Role Alignment

## The role, translated into delivery outcomes

| What the role asks for | How I would apply it | Relevant CV evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Manage multiple programmes / workstreams | One integrated plan with workstream owners, dependencies and decision points | Concurrent transformation engagements across discovery, implementation, rollout, adoption and optimisation |
| Maintain plans, milestones, actions, risks and dependencies | Weekly integrated delivery view + RAID + ageing actions | Governance, delivery assurance, RAID, risk/dependency management |
| Build dashboards and KPI reporting | Programme health, engagement, cycle-time and outcome metrics | Power BI, KPI frameworks, executive dashboards and reporting packs |
| Manage stakeholder communication and governance | Clear cadence by audience: delivery, client, executive | Boards, clients, IT, operations, suppliers, public/private stakeholders |
| Analyse adoption, engagement and performance | Trend views linked to next actions, not passive reporting | Data-led reporting, CRM/pipeline visibility, operational performance tracking |
| Improve programme effectiveness | Identify bottlenecks, standardise hand-offs, set SLAs | Process redesign, workflow optimisation, automation and change adoption |
| Support GTM and customer engagement | Connect credible customer evidence to sales/marketing activity | CRM transformation, customer workflows, business development and commercial improvement |
| Technology environment | Use enough technical depth to translate between business and delivery teams | Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho, M365, Copilot, SQL, Python, automation, AI-enabled workflows |

## My strongest fit

**I combine structured programme delivery with technology, data, operations and customer/commercial understanding.**

That combination is important in a GTM programme because the Programme Manager is not simply tracking tasks. The role has to keep customer activity, marketing outputs, sales needs, data, approvals and operational dependencies moving together.
