# 30-60-90 Day Approach

## Days 0-30 - Learn & Map
- Understand client objectives, programme scope and success measures.
- Map active workstreams, owners, stakeholders and decision routes.
- Review data sources, dashboards, reporting cadence and existing RAID.
- Understand customer-engagement and approval journeys end to end.
- Identify immediate delivery risks and ageing decisions.

**Output:** clear baseline of programme health, stakeholders, dependencies and priority gaps.

## Days 31-60 - Stabilise & Align
- Strengthen the integrated programme plan.
- Standardise RAG, RAID, milestone and action tracking.
- Confirm governance rhythm and escalation thresholds.
- Establish a concise KPI model covering activity, flow, engagement and outcomes.
- Improve hand-offs between customer engagement, content, operations and GTM teams.

**Output:** one trusted view of delivery with clear ownership and faster decisions.

## Days 61-90 - Improve & Scale
- Analyse bottlenecks, repeat delays and approval cycle time.
- Introduce practical SLAs / workflow improvements where useful.
- Automate repeat reporting steps where feasible.
- Link programme reporting more clearly to customer and commercial outcomes.
- Build an optimisation backlog for the next quarter.

**Output:** a programme that is easier to run, easier to understand and more scalable.
