# 30-60-90 Day GTM Programme Approach

## Days 0-30: Learn, Map and Baseline

- Understand programme objectives, target audiences and success measures.
- Map active workstreams across customer advocacy, peer engagement, content, video, events, sales enablement and reporting.
- Identify key stakeholders, decision-makers, suppliers and approval routes.
- Review the current integrated plan, RAID, reporting cadence and KPI definitions.
- Understand the customer journey from sourcing and qualification through approval and activation.
- Identify ageing actions, recurring bottlenecks and immediate delivery risks.

**Output:** a clear baseline of programme health, stakeholder ownership, dependencies and priority issues.

---

## Days 31-60: Stabilise and Align 
- Strengthen the integrated programme plan and milestone view.
- Standardise RAG, RAID, dependency and action tracking.
- Confirm governance cadence and escalation thresholds.
- Introduce or refine KPI reporting across engagement, cycle time, content production, activation and outcomes.
- Improve hand-offs between customer engagement, content, production, events, sales and reporting teams.
- Clarify approval SLAs and ownership where delays are recurring.
- Establish one trusted view of programme status for both delivery and leadership stakeholders.

**Output:** a more controlled programme with clearer ownership, stronger visibility and faster decision-making.

---

## Days 61-90: Improve and Scale

- Analyse recurring delays, approval cycle time and delivery friction.
- Optimise workflows and standardise repeatable processes.
- Automate repeat reporting steps where practical.
- Improve capacity planning across regional launches, customer activity and production demand.
- Link programme reporting more clearly to customer, sales and commercial outcomes.
- Build an optimisation backlog for the next quarter.
- Identify opportunities to scale successful workflows across markets.

**Output:** a programme that is easier to run, easier to understand and better connected to measurable GTM outcomes.

---

## My focus throughout

**Plan → Align → Deliver → Track → Resolve → Measure → Improve**

The objective is not simply to keep activity moving. It is to keep every workstream connected to the customer experience, programme objective and measurable business outcome.
