# Selected STAR Case Studies

Short examples showing how I approach delivery, governance, stakeholders and measurable outcomes.

## 1. UK Public Sector Technology Programme

**Situation**  
Through a UK organisation, I was contracted to support a public-sector laptop refresh programme for Westminster City Council. The programme required reliable visibility across technology, operations and procurement.

**Task**  
Support programme governance, reporting, deployment planning and operational coordination.

**Action**  
I developed programme dashboards and governance reporting packs covering rollout progress, risks, dependencies and performance. I worked across IT, operations and procurement to validate information, surface issues early and support delivery assurance.

**Result**  
Programme leads had clearer visibility of status, risks and decision-critical information.

**Relevant to:** governance • dashboards • RAID • stakeholder coordination • technology delivery

## 2. Trinvisor Solutions

**Situation**

Keep client initiatives moving from discovery and business case through implementation, rollout, adoption and optimisation.

**Action**

**Result**
