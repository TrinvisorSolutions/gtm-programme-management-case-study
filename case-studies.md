# Selected STAR Case Studies

Short examples selected because they map directly to the role.

## 1. UK Public Sector Technology Programme - programme visibility and governance

**Situation**  
Through a UK organisation, I was contracted to support a public-sector laptop refresh programme for Westminster City Council. The programme required reliable delivery visibility across technology, operations and procurement.

**Task**  
Support governance, reporting, deployment planning and operational coordination so programme leads could see progress, risks and dependencies clearly.

**Action**  
I developed programme dashboards and governance reporting packs covering rollout progress, risks, dependencies and performance. I worked across IT, operations and procurement to validate information, surface issues and support delivery assurance.

**Result**  
The programme had clearer, more consistent visibility of delivery status and decision-critical information.

**Role link:** dashboards • governance • risk/dependency management • stakeholder coordination • technology delivery

## 2. Trinvisor Solutions - concurrent transformation workstreams

**Situation**  
My consultancy supported 250+ active clients across the UK, Europe and Caribbean, with transformation engagements running concurrently.

**Task**  
Keep multiple client initiatives moving from discovery and business case through implementation, rollout, adoption and optimisation.

**Action**  
I structured engagements around objectives, owners, dependencies and implementation readiness; coordinated clients and suppliers; established governance and reporting; and led work spanning CRM, process redesign, data migration, automation and digital tooling.

**Result**  
The consultancy scaled while maintaining structured delivery across multiple simultaneous engagements, with improved workflow efficiency, reporting effort and pipeline visibility in CRM programmes.

**Role link:** multiple workstreams • client delivery • prioritisation • CRM • automation • GTM/customer workflows

## 3. Therml Impac Group - transformation across complex operations

**Situation**  
A large manufacturing, engineering and construction group needed more consistent processes, reporting and technology adoption across functions and sites.

**Task**  
Lead operational and digital transformation while maintaining board-level visibility and coordinating cross-functional teams.

**Action**  
I led multi-site process standardisation, workflow redesign and technology adoption; implemented governance and performance-tracking mechanisms; coordinated functions including engineering, IT, HR, operations and marketing; and managed suppliers and business-critical initiatives within time and budget constraints.

**Result**  
The organisation improved operational consistency and strengthened board-level oversight and data-led decision-making.

**Role link:** transformation • governance • stakeholder management • process improvement • adoption • commercial delivery

## How I use STAR in interview

**Situation 15% → Task 15% → Action 50% → Result 20%**

I keep the emphasis on what I personally did, the decision logic behind it, and what changed as a result.
